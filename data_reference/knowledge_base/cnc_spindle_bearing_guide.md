# CNC Spindle Bearing Degradation Guide

Document ID: DOC-CNC-BRG-001
Version: 1.0.0
Knowledge domain: FAILURE_MODE_GUIDE
Machine type: CNC

## Detection context

Use rising vibration together with increasing spindle or machine temperature and abnormal power demand as evidence of possible spindle bearing degradation.

Do not diagnose bearing failure from a single telemetry reading. Compare the current observation with the recent trend and the machine operating state.

## Inspection guidance

Verify that the machine is in an appropriate safe state before physical inspection. Follow the site's isolation and lockout/tagout procedure.

Inspect for abnormal bearing noise, visible contamination, lubrication condition and evidence of mechanical looseness.

Compare vibration and temperature against the machine's maintenance baseline and the current operating load.

## Escalation

Escalate to Reliability Engineering when multiple symptoms persist or when the predictive-maintenance risk is HIGH or CRITICAL.

The RAG assistant must not instruct an operator to bypass a guard, interlock, protective device or safety procedure.
