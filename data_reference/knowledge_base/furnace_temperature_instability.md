# Furnace Temperature Instability Guide

Document ID: DOC-FRN-TMP-001
Version: 1.0.0
Knowledge domain: FAILURE_MODE_GUIDE
Machine type: FURNACE

## Indicators

Unexpected chamber temperature movement, abnormal fuel flow or process pressure can indicate thermal or process instability.

Trend the temperature rather than relying on a single reading. Consider current workload, recipe and operating state.

## Inspection guidance

Follow the furnace-specific safe operating and isolation procedure before physical inspection.

Verify approved recipe parameters, sensor status and recent alarm history.

Do not bypass temperature protection or process safety controls.

## Escalation

Escalate repeated instability, critical alarms or predictive-maintenance risk to the responsible maintenance and process engineering teams.
