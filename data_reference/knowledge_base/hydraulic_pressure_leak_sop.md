# Hydraulic Pressure Leak SOP

Document ID: DOC-HPR-LEAK-001
Version: 1.0.0
Knowledge domain: STANDARD_OPERATING_PROCEDURE
Machine type: HYDRAULIC_PRESS

## Indicators

A pressure decline accompanied by reduced force or abnormal cycle behavior may indicate a hydraulic pressure leak.

Corroborate telemetry with the current operating state and recent alarm history.

## Safe inspection

Stop or isolate the affected equipment according to the approved site procedure before opening, loosening or inspecting hydraulic components.

Check for visible fluid leakage, damaged hoses, loose fittings and abnormal reservoir level.

Do not touch pressurized hydraulic components.

## Escalation

Create or update the maintenance review through the plant's approved workflow. Do not return the machine to production until required inspection and verification steps are completed.
