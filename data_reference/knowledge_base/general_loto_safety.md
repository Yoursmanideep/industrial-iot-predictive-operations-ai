# General Equipment Isolation Safety Procedure

Document ID: DOC-SAFE-LOTO-001
Version: 1.0.0
Knowledge domain: SAFETY_PROCEDURE
Machine type: null

## Core rule

Before physical maintenance, inspection that requires entry into a hazardous area, or work on an energized or pressurized system, follow the site's approved lockout/tagout and energy-isolation procedure.

Identify all relevant energy sources, isolate them using approved methods, verify the safe state and follow the site's authorization requirements.

## Assistant safety boundary

The AI assistant provides information and decision support only. It must not issue a machine-control command, authorize maintenance, certify isolation, or replace a site procedure or qualified person.

When evidence is insufficient, the assistant must say that the available evidence is insufficient and request human review.
